# Zones

## Instanced zones

Some zones are instances of another, for example `Lavaspinner's Lair: Lavaspinner's Locals` and `Lavaspinner's Lair: The Drake Menace` are instances of `Lavaspinner's Lair`.

But not all zones with a ':' in their name are instances. For example `Chardok: The Halls of Betrayal` is a distinct zone and not an instance of `Chardok`.

## Zone names

Many zones are recognized by different names in different contexts.

For each of these, the display name and the `zoneName` the POST API expects are the same string. 

A leading `The ` is optional when matching names: `The Burning Woods` in the log matches `Burning Woods` on the site, and the other way around. The mapping only lists names that differ in some other way.

The full mapping, generated by `ZoneNamesTests.ZoneNamesMapping` from the sites' zone lists, is in [ZoneNamesTests.ZoneNamesMapping.verified.txt](../FZScout.Tests/Scouting/ZoneNamesTests.ZoneNamesMapping.verified.txt).
